# Vendor and Third-Party Management Policy

## Vendor Risk Assessment
Before onboarding, all third-party vendors and subprocessors that handle customer
data undergo a security risk assessment. The assessment reviews the vendor's
security certifications (such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001), data handling practices, and
subprocessing arrangements. Vendors are re-assessed at least annually.

## Contractual Safeguards
All vendors that process customer or personal data are required to sign a data
protection agreement (DPA) and contractual security clauses covering
confidentiality, breach notification, and data return or deletion on termination.

## Subprocessor Management
A current list of subprocessors is maintained and made available to customers.
Customers are notified before a new subprocessor that handles their data is
engaged, and may object in line with contractual terms.

## Ongoing Monitoring
Vendor security posture is monitored throughout the relationship. Critical vendors
are reviewed for continued compliance, and any material security incident at a
vendor triggers a reassessment.
