# Data Retention and Disposal Policy

## Retention Periods
Customer data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service and to
meet legal obligations. Operational customer data is retained for the duration of
the active contract. Application and security logs are retained for 12 months.

## Deletion After Contract Termination
Following termination of a customer contract, customer production data is deleted
within 30 days. Encrypted backups containing customer data are purged within 90
days as they age out of the backup rotation. Customers may request earlier
deletion in writing.

## Secure Disposal
Data is securely disposed of using cryptographic erasure (destruction of the
encryption keys) and, for physical media, degaussing or shredding performed by a
certified vendor. Certificates of destruction are retained for audit purposes.
