# Incident Response Policy

## Incident Response Plan
Acme Cloud Inc. maintains a documented incident response plan that is reviewed
annually and tested at least once per year through a tabletop exercise. The plan
defines roles, communication paths, and escalation procedures for security events.

## Incident Classification and Escalation
Security incidents are classified by severity (SEV1 critical, SEV2 high, SEV3
moderate, SEV4 low) based on impact to confidentiality, integrity, and
availability. SEV1 and SEV2 incidents are escalated immediately to the on-call
security lead and the incident commander, who coordinate containment and
remediation.

## Breach Notification
In the event of a confirmed breach of customer data, affected customers are
notified without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours of confirmation.
Notifications include the nature of the incident, data affected, and remediation
steps. Regulatory authorities are notified as required by applicable law.

## Logging and Monitoring
Security-relevant events are centrally logged and monitored 24/7. Alerts are
triaged by the security operations team, and logs are retained for at least 12
months to support investigation.
